About Me

Hi, I’m Calvin Yang. Looks like you want to know more about me! Well for starters, I'm born and raised in Mississauga, and I've always had a passion for a variety of games, from competitive shooters and horror games to farming and heartfelt story games.

I enjoy experimenting across tools and engines like Unity, Unreal, and spreadsheet simulations to organize and layout my entire life. My projects often explore small mechanics with big expressive potential, making games that feel alive through the choices players make.

When I'm not designing, you can find me sketching concepts, fixating on a random personal projects, or playing my favorite game, League of Legends.
