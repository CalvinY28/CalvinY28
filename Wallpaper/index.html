These two wallpaper were made by yours truly in the summer of 2024, a little solo project, each taking around a month of time. The tools used are Unity, Clip Studio Paint, Aseprite, and Wallpaper Engine.

My goals for this project were to expand my grasp of Unity and pixel art. My motivations came from some of the pixel wallpapers that Wallpaper Engine gives out on install.
